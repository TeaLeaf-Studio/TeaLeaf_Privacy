Privacy Policy — Chicken's Life

Effective: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Chicken's Life app ("the game") handles, and why. The game is made by Tea Leaf Studio ("we", "us"). Questions: nostagiagames21@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect any personal information on our own servers. We do not run any servers.
  • Your farm, your chickens and your settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.
  • The optional purchases ("Remove ads", the Starter Bundle and gene upgrades) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

The PC version (itch.io)

The Windows and Linux version sold on itch.io has no ads, no in-app purchases and no network features. It saves your progress only in your own user folder on your computer, and sends nothing to us or anyone else. Purchases on itch.io are handled by itch.io under its own privacy policy (https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy). The rest of this policy describes the Android version.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in files on your device: your farm and birds, your sound setting, which tips you have seen, whether you have removed ads, and any gene upgrades you have paid for but not yet used. These files never leave your device. We cannot see them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads between races, served by Google AdMob, a Google service. After training or a race you can also choose to watch a "rewarded" ad to double what you won; these are only ever shown when you ask for one. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google (not us) may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps);
  • diagnostic information about ad performance.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the AD PRIVACY button in the game's pause menu.
  • Everywhere, you can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android devices).
  • The "Remove ads" purchase turns off all ads in the game, except the rewarded ads you choose to watch.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases ("Remove ads", the Starter Bundle and gene upgrades) are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether your account owns "Remove ads" or the Starter Bundle, so that it can turn ads off, including after a reinstall or on a new device, and whether a gene upgrade has been paid for, so that it can deliver it.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us at the address above and we will help you remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you. Information on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. To manage information held by Google, including ad data, see Google's controls at https://myaccount.google.com and in your device's ad settings.

Security

Information sent by the ads service and by Google Play travels over encrypted connections operated by Google.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Where a change is significant, we will say so in the game's store listing or release notes.
